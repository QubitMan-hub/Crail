# How CRAIL gets built

CRAIL started as one cube. Everything else is decided by the people watching.

## The cycle

1. A Reel goes up on [@qubitman_ftw](https://www.instagram.com/qubitman_ftw/) showing the game as it is today.
2. Voting is open for **24 hours** from the moment the Reel is posted.
3. The winning idea is built and added to the game.
4. Two days after the last Reel, the next one shows the result, and voting opens again.

## Two kinds of round

Each Reel says which kind of round it is.

- **Top comment.** The top-level comment with the most likes when voting closes wins.
- **Poll.** A poll offers a few options, and the option with the most votes when it closes wins.

## Creator's pick

On some days the creator also picks **one extra idea** from the comments, on top of the winner. The pick is for ideas that make the game better to actually play, which the like count does not always reward. The Reel before says when a pick is coming, the top comment still wins as usual, and the picked idea is credited to its commenter in exactly the same way.

## Top-comment rounds

- One idea per comment. Replies do not count as entries.
- Likes are counted once, at the deadline, using Instagram's official data. Likes after that do not count.
- **Ties:** the comment posted first wins.
- The creator's own comments cannot win.
- The same person can win as many times as the community lets them.

## Poll rounds

- The result is read from Instagram when the poll closes and recorded with a screenshot.
- **Ties:** the tied options go to a top-comment round on the next Reel.

## When the winner cannot be built as written

- **Too big for two days:** it is not skipped. The smallest version that keeps the spirit of the idea is built, or it is split across several episodes.
- **Technically impossible:** the next-ranked idea wins.
- **Not allowed:** the next-ranked idea wins. Not allowed means hateful, sexual or harassing content, anything aimed at a real person, real-money or gambling mechanics, copyrighted characters or copies of existing games, and anything that breaks Instagram's rules.
- **Suspicious likes:** a comment whose likes are clearly bought or botted is disqualified.

Whenever the top idea is not the one that gets built, the reason is said out loud in the next Reel and written in the ledger.

## The record

Every round has a file in [`ledger/`](ledger/): the ranking at the deadline, the winner, and the reasoning. Every feature in the game carries the name of the person who asked for it and their exact words.
